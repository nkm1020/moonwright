# Habitat Berm and Foundation Pit in Four Days

**Site.** Firm regolith 400 m north of the landing pad with 25 cm of fines; the berm line is 20 m south of the pit. The depot is on the east edge; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Foundation pit: 10 m by 10 m, 0.8 m deep, base levelled and compacted.
2. Plume berm: 60 m straight berm, 2 m high, from pit spoil plus a borrow strip, compacted in two lifts.
3. Borrow strip: 40 m by 5 m, 0.4 m deep, next to the berm line.

**Fleet.** EX1 and EX2 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), HL1 and HL2 (haulers, 3000 Wh each), CP1 and CP2 (compactors, 3500 Wh each) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 4 days. A crew lander arrives on day five and the berm must be complete.

**Operational constraints.**

- Both excavators and both compactors must work in parallel; keep the critical path short.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
- The surveyor stakes the pit and the berm line before excavation and inspects the berm at the end.
