# LS-1 Solar Array Pad

**Site.** LS-1 plateau, 300 m west of the landing pad, on a gentle 2 degree slope of loose regolith 30 cm deep over compacted material. The depot is at the south edge of the work area and there is no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Solar array pad: a level rectangle 30 m by 18 m. Strip the loose regolith (30 cm of fines, about 162 cubic metres), cut the uphill side and fill the downhill side to within plus or minus 5 cm, then compact the whole 540 square metre surface in two full lifts.
2. Anchor trench: a 30 m long, 0.5 m wide, 0.5 m deep trench along the north edge for the array anchor rail, backfilled and compacted after the rail bed is inspected.

**Fleet.** EX1 (excavator, 5000 Wh), HL1 (hauler, 3000 Wh), CP1 (compactor, 3500 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Each battery is the budget for the whole job.

**Deadline.** 10 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- The array vendor requires both compaction lifts over the full pad area; neither the pad size nor the number of lifts may be reduced, and no second compactor is available.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
- The surveyor stakes the pad corners before any excavation and checks the level before the second compaction lift.
- Cut spoil goes to the fill side first; only the surplus is hauled to the stockpile.
