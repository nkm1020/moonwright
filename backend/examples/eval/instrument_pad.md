# Instrument Pad

**Site.** A 20 m by 20 m patch of firm regolith 150 m north of the landing pad. Surface fines are 15 cm deep. Depot to the south; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Instrument pad: 10 m by 10 m, stripped of fines, levelled to plus or minus 3 cm and compacted, with four staked anchor points.

**Fleet.** EX1 (excavator, 5000 Wh), CP1 (compactor, 3500 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh).

**Deadline.** 3 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- The surveyor stakes the pad before stripping and certifies the level after compaction.
- Only one robot may work inside the pad at a time.
