# Twin Cargo Landing Pads

**Site.** A flat 120 m by 60 m area of loose regolith 30 cm deep, 500 m south of the main landing pad. The two pads are 60 m apart. The depot is between them; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Cargo pad A: 15 m diameter, excavated 0.4 m to firm regolith, compacted subgrade, sintered 25 cm surface.
2. Cargo pad B: identical to pad A.
3. Shared blast berm: a 40 m straight berm between the pads, 2 m high, built from pad spoil and compacted, crest sintered.

**Fleet.** EX1 and EX2 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), HL1 and HL2 (haulers, 3000 Wh each), CP1 and CP2 (compactors, 3500 Wh each), PR1 (printer, 4000 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are not recharged.

**Deadline.** 16 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- The single printer sinters pad A, then pad B, then the berm crest; it never works while an excavator is in the same zone.
- Each pad is staked by the surveyor before excavation and inspected after sintering.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
