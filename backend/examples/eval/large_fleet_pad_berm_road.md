# Main Landing Pad, Berm and Access Road with an Eleven-Robot Fleet

**Site.** LS-1 plateau, loose regolith 40 cm deep over firm material across a 150 m by 100 m area. Depot on the south edge; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Landing pad: 30 m diameter, excavated 0.4 m (about 283 cubic metres), compacted subgrade, sintered 30 cm surface over the full 707 square metres.
2. Blast berm: 90 m arc, 2.5 m high and 5 m wide at the base, from pad spoil, compacted, with the crest sintered 1 m wide.
3. Access road: 100 m long, 5 m wide, graded and compacted from the depot to the pad.

**Fleet.** EX1, EX2 and EX3 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), HL1, HL2 and HL3 (haulers, 3000 Wh each), CP1 and CP2 (compactors, 3500 Wh each), PR1 and PR2 (printers, 4000 Wh each) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 12 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- Split the pad excavation between the three excavators by sector so they never share a zone.
- The road is finished before pad spoil is hauled to the berm along it.
- Compaction and sintering must be shared evenly between the two compactors and the two printers: the pad surface alone is close to one printer's whole battery.
- The printers sinter the pad before the berm crest; the surveyor inspects all three structures.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
