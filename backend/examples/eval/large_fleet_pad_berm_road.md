# Main Landing Pad, Berm and Access Road with a Ten-Robot Fleet

**Site.** LS-1 plateau, loose regolith 40 cm deep over firm material across a 150 m by 100 m area. Depot on the south edge; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Landing pad: 30 m diameter, excavated 0.4 m, compacted subgrade, sintered 30 cm surface.
2. Blast berm: 90 m arc, 2.5 m high, from pad spoil, compacted, crest sintered.
3. Access road: 100 m long, 5 m wide, graded and compacted from the depot to the pad.

**Fleet.** EX1, EX2 and EX3 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), HL1, HL2 and HL3 (haulers, 3000 Wh each), CP1 and CP2 (compactors, 3500 Wh each), PR1 (printer, 4000 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 12 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- Split the pad excavation between the three excavators by sector so they never share a zone.
- The road is finished before pad spoil is hauled to the berm along it.
- The printer sinters the pad before the berm crest; the surveyor inspects all three structures.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
