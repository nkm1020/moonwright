# Propellant Storage Pit and Berm

**Site.** Firm regolith 600 m east of the landing pad with a 25 cm layer of fines. The pit and its berm sit 15 m apart. The depot is on the west side; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Storage pit: 8 m diameter, 2 m deep, with a 1:1 sloped wall, floor compacted and a sintered 20 cm floor slab.
2. Protective berm: a 30 m arc on the pad side of the pit, 2.5 m high, built from pit spoil and compacted.
3. Haul lane: a 40 m by 5 m stripped and compacted lane from the depot to the pit.

**Fleet.** EX1 and EX2 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), HL1 and HL2 (haulers, 3000 Wh each), CP1 (compactor, 3500 Wh), PR1 (printer, 4000 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 10 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- The haul lane is finished before pit excavation starts.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time; the pit is one zone, the berm another.
- The surveyor inspects the pit floor before the slab is printed and the berm after compaction.
