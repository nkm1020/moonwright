# Landing Pad Bowl with Depleted Batteries

**Site.** The LS-1 landing pad footprint, loose regolith 40 cm deep over firm material, depot on the south edge. The fleet arrives with partly depleted batteries and there is no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Landing pad bowl: 20 m diameter, excavated 0.5 m to firm regolith (about 157 cubic metres over the full footprint), subgrade compacted. Sintering is a later job.
2. Spoil stockpile: pad spoil placed in a compact pile 30 m north of the pad, shaped so it can later become berm fill.

**Fleet.** EX1 (excavator, 1800 Wh remaining), HL1 (hauler, 2000 Wh remaining), CP1 (compactor, 2000 Wh remaining) and SV1 (surveyor, 1000 Wh remaining). These remaining charges are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 7 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- Plan only what the remaining batteries can actually finish; a smaller first phase that reaches firm regolith over the central 12 m (or any diameter between 12 m and 20 m that fits) is acceptable if the full bowl does not fit, and the stockpile and compaction shrink with it.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
- The surveyor checks the bowl depth before compaction.
