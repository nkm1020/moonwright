# Power Cable Trench to the Habitat Site

**Site.** A 150 m corridor from the solar array pad to the habitat foundation, across loose regolith with scattered cobbles. The route crosses one shallow 10 m wide depression. The depot is at the array end; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Cable trench: 150 m long, 0.4 m wide, 0.6 m deep, cut in three 50 m segments so the cable crew can follow behind, with a compacted 10 cm bedding layer in each segment before the cable goes in.
2. Depression crossing: a 10 m earth bridge of compacted fill across the depression so the trench keeps a steady depth.

**Fleet.** EX1 (excavator, 5000 Wh), HL1 (hauler, 3000 Wh), CP1 (compactor, 3500 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Each battery is the whole job's budget.

**Deadline.** 5 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- Segments are cut in order from the array end; the compactor beds a segment only after the surveyor has checked its depth.
- The earth bridge is built before segment two is cut through the depression.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
