# Regolith Feedstock Stockpile for ISRU

**Site.** A regolith borrow field 200 m west of the ISRU plant, loose material 50 cm deep over firm ground, with the stockpile pad next to the plant. The depot is at the plant; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Borrow excavation: about 200 cubic metres of loose regolith taken from three 20 m by 10 m strips, 0.35 m deep each.
2. Feedstock stockpile: the excavated material placed on the plant's stockpile pad in a windrow 25 m long, lightly compacted so it does not blow away during plant start-up.

**Fleet.** EX1 (excavator, 5000 Wh), HL1 and HL2 (haulers, 3000 Wh each), CP1 (compactor, 3500 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 8 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- Each strip is surveyed before excavation so the plant can log the source material.
- Haulers alternate strips so the excavator is never waiting for a hauler.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
