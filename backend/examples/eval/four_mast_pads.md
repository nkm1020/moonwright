# Four Communication Mast Pads

**Site.** Four corners of a 100 m square around the habitat area, each on firm regolith with 20 cm of fines. The depot is at the centre of the square; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Mast pad NE, 2. mast pad NW, 3. mast pad SE, 4. mast pad SW: each 8 m by 8 m, stripped of fines, levelled, compacted, with a 1 m deep central anchor hole excavated and backfilled with compacted regolith around the anchor.

**Fleet.** EX1 and EX2 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), HL1 (hauler, 3000 Wh), CP1 and CP2 (compactors, 3500 Wh each) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 6 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- The four pads are independent and should proceed in parallel wherever the fleet allows.
- Each pad is staked before stripping and inspected after the anchor backfill.
- Only one robot may work inside a pad at a time.
