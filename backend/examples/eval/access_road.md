# LS-1 Access Road and Habitat Foundation

**Site.** LS-1 plateau, the same site as the landing pad. The route runs 120 m west from the pad apron across level regolith to the habitat site and climbs less than 2 m. The surface is fine regolith over a firm layer at 30 cm, with no boulders larger than 0.5 m along the surveyed corridor. The depot is at the pad end of the route.

**Structures.**

1. Access road: 120 m long, 4 m wide, graded and compacted regolith with a 20 cm compacted lift, crowned to shed dust, built as two 60 m segments.
2. Habitat foundation: 8 m by 8 m, excavated 0.5 m to firm regolith and compacted level for the habitat module.
3. Protective berm: 20 m long, 1.5 m high, along the north edge of the foundation, built from foundation spoil.

**Fleet.** EX1 (excavator, 5000 Wh), HL1 (hauler, 3000 Wh), CP1 and CP2 (compactors, 3500 Wh each) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). No printer is available for this sortie, so nothing is sintered. No recharging.

**Deadline.** 10 days from mobilisation.

**Operational constraints.**

- Grade the road from the pad end towards the habitat so haulers always have a finished surface behind them.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
- The surveyor inspects the road before the foundation is signed off.
