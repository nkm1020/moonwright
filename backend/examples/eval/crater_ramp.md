# Access Ramp into Crater C-4

**Site.** The rim of crater C-4, 6 m above the crater floor, 1.2 km from the landing pad. The wall is loose regolith over blocky material. The depot is on the rim; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Access ramp: 40 m long and 5 m wide, cut into the wall at a 15 percent grade in four 10 m segments from the top down, with the cut from each segment used as fill for the segment below.
2. Rim turnaround: a level 10 m by 10 m apron at the top of the ramp, compacted so haulers can turn.
3. Floor landing: a compacted 8 m by 8 m pad at the foot of the ramp.

**Fleet.** EX1 and EX2 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), HL1 (hauler, 3000 Wh), CP1 (compactor, 3500 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 8 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- Segments are cut strictly from the top down; a segment is compacted before the next one is opened.
- The surveyor checks the grade of every segment before compaction.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
