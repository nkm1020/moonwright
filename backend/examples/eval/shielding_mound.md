# Regolith Radiation Shielding over Habitat Module A

**Site.** Habitat Module A is already in place on its foundation, 10 m long and 4 m in diameter, on firm ground 400 m north of the landing pad. A borrow area of loose regolith lies 80 m to the west. The depot is at the borrow area; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Shielding mound: cover the module with 1.5 m of regolith on the sides and 2 m on top, about 120 cubic metres of fill, placed in three compacted lifts from a borrow pit.
2. Borrow pit: a 15 m by 10 m excavation 1 m deep that supplies the fill; its floor is graded afterwards so it can serve as a future storage pad.

**Fleet.** EX1 (excavator, 5000 Wh), HL1, HL2 and HL3 (haulers, 3000 Wh each), CP1 (compactor, 3500 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Each battery is the budget for the whole job.

**Deadline.** 9 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- Each lift is compacted before the next lift is placed; the surveyor checks the final thickness.
- Haulers do not enter the mound zone while the compactor is working in it.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
