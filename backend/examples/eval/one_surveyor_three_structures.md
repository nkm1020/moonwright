# Three Structures, One Surveyor

**Site.** The LS-1 plateau south area: firm regolith with 25 cm of fines across a 100 m by 60 m strip. Depot at the centre of the south edge; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Equipment hardstand: 12 m by 12 m, stripped of its 25 cm of fines, levelled and compacted (not sintered).
2. Spoil containment berm: 40 m long, 1.5 m high, built from the hardstand's strip spoil and compacted.
3. Rover charging apron: 10 m by 20 m, stripped, compacted and sintered 15 cm.

**Fleet.** EX1 and EX2 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), HL1 (hauler, 3000 Wh), CP1 (compactor, 3500 Wh), PR1 (printer, 4000 Wh) and SV1 (surveyor, 1200 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 9 days.

**Operational constraints.**

- Every structure is staked by the surveyor before earthwork starts and inspected by the surveyor after its last task; the single surveyor's battery must cover all six visits.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
- The printer works only after the apron has been compacted and inspected.
