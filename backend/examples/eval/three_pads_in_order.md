# Three Cargo Pads Before the First Lander

**Site.** A flat 120 m by 60 m area of loose regolith 40 cm deep over firm material, 500 m south of the main landing pad. The three pads are 40 m apart along an east-west line. The depot is on the south edge, midway; no recharging.

**Structures.**

1. Cargo pad A, 2. cargo pad B, 3. cargo pad C: each a circular bowl 14 m in diameter excavated 0.4 m to firm regolith (about 62 cubic metres each), subgrade compacted, staked before excavation and inspected after compaction. The spoil of each pad is bladed into a low windrow on its north side by the excavator; nothing is hauled and nothing is sintered this campaign.

**Fleet.** EX1 and EX2 (excavators, 5000 Wh each), CP1 and CP2 (compactors, 3500 Wh each) and SV1 (surveyor, 1500 Wh). Batteries are the whole budget.

**Deadline.** 2 days. The first cargo lander arrives at the start of day three and all three pads must be inspected by then.

**Operational constraints.**

- The pad team prefers to finish each pad completely before the next one is opened (pad A, then B, then C), so that only one zone is active at a time; this preference yields to the deadline, and the three pads are independent, so they may be worked in parallel on separate zones if the deadline requires it.
- Only one robot may work inside a zone at a time.
- The surveyor stakes a pad before its excavation and inspects it after its compaction.
